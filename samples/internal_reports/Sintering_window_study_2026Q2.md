# Sintering window study — Q2 2026 (internal)

Prepared by the CellLoop materials team. Fictional data for demonstration.

## Summary

Across 14 metal-supported button cells co-sintered in reducing atmosphere (2% H2/Ar), total ASR at 650 °C was lowest for sintering temperatures between 1220 °C and 1260 °C. Below 1150 °C the cathode/electrolyte interface was poorly bonded and polarization ASR rose above 0.35 Ω·cm². Above 1300 °C we observed Ni coarsening in the anode and onset of support oxidation, which increased both ohmic and polarization resistance.

## Dwell time

Dwell times of 2 to 3 hours performed best. A 6 hour dwell at 1250 °C increased polarization ASR by roughly 15% relative to a 2 hour dwell, consistent with cathode particle coarsening.

## Recommendation

Hold the sintering window at 1230 ± 20 °C with a 2–3 h dwell for the next design round, and focus remaining experiments on electrolyte thickness and coating thickness, which the Gaussian-process model ranks as the most influential remaining variables.
