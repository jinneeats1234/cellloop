# MS-SOFC program targets and milestones — FY26 (internal)

Fictional data for demonstration.

## Cell-level performance targets

- Total area-specific resistance (ASR) at or below 0.30 Ω·cm² at 650 °C in 97% H2 / 3% H2O versus air.
- Peak power density of at least 0.9 W/cm² at 650 °C.
- Degradation below 1.5 %/kh over 1,000 hours.
- Survive 100 thermal cycles between 200 °C and 650 °C without delamination.

## Process targets

- Partner results structured and searchable within 48 hours of submission.
- At least 90% of experiment records with complete metadata.
- 25% fewer experiment cycles to reach the ASR target than the historical baseline of about 40 cycles.

## Milestones

The stack design review is scheduled after two consecutive cell builds meet the ASR target with fewer than 2 %/kh degradation. Electrolyte thinning below 5 µm is the main lever under investigation by the Ridgeline and Northgate partners.
